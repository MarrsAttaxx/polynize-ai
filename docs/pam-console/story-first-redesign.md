## 1. The honest diagnosis

### 1a. What you said, and what is actually true

**"It doesn't create scripts."** It does create scripts, and until yesterday they were bad for a structural reason: one AI call wrote the whole piece from a brief that measured about 1% of its instructions, and you had no say between typing and receiving. The staged build (agree hooks, agree the arc, then write) shipped yesterday and you have not touched it yet. So: half true, fixed on paper, unproven in your hands.

**"It can't edit videos."** True, and it never could. Editing lives outside: Final Cut, Descript, the local render pipeline. The console's real failure is that it pretended otherwise. The flow went script, record, then nothing. No hand-off out, no way back in. A hole in the middle of the pipe, denied instead of designed.

**"It can't create Prezies."** The tool exists and is unreliable, because it tried to be an infinite conversational design tool, which is the hardest possible version of the problem. Meanwhile the marketing plan says your artefact is always one of about five things: a map, a matrix, a split, a number, a teardown. Five reliable templates beat infinite unreliable drawing.

### 1b. The six root causes

**1. The unit of work was wrong.** The console's atom is a "piece": one post. Your atom is a Story exploited across every platform. So the console produced orphans, and the campaign only existed in your head.

**2. Nothing defined "done" as PUBLISHED.** Every module optimised for creating. The publishing end (calendar, Metricool) shipped and then sat untested at the end of a pipe nothing flows down. A content machine whose definition of done is "draft exists" is a drafts machine.

**3. Choice points everywhere.** Pick a stream. Pick a template. Pick an angle. Pick a format. Every choice is a place momentum dies, and ADHD does not need more options, it needs a track. We built a menu when you needed a conveyor.

**4. Breadth always beat depth.** Five streams, ten-plus modules, dozens of formats, before one stream shipped one story end to end. A tool that tries to do everything for every person gives each feature one afternoon, instead of giving one flow every afternoon.

**5. The missing middle.** The edit is external, but the console had no concept of "waiting on the edit" or "the edit came back". Every video flow dead-ended exactly where your hardest work happens.

**6. Verification debt.** The user manual has modules marked "shipped, pending human test" that stayed that way for weeks. 36 of the 40 tests in the testing checklist were never run. Shipping is not working.

### 1c. My share of it

You said your pattern is "more and more". It was our pattern. You asked, I built, same day, every time, and told you it shipped. I never once said "no, not until the last thing is proven". The fix for that is not a mood, it is a rule, and it is in section 7.

> **Show the work.** Every piece of content carries a real artefact: a map, a matrix, a split, a number, a teardown. Every piece should be something only we could have made.

That rule is a gift. It means the console does not need to make "content". It needs to make **artefacts, and the words around them**.

## 3. The redesign

### 3a. The unit: a Story, not a concept

A core concept was a big baggy document you never re-read. Your words: "it's kind of a big document, I'm not really taking note of what's in that." A **Story** is a structured sheet with named slots and a fill meter. A recipe card, not an essay. If ingredients are missing, the console says which ones and refuses to bake.

### 3b. The Story sheet

| Slot | What goes in it | Example from 3 Types |
|---|---|---|
| Premise | One line | Three types of people are emerging in the AI economy, and only one survives |
| Layer | Belief / Wedge / Entry (the plan's strategic frame) | Belief |
| Job | Attract (lead-gen language) or Convert (buying language) | Attract |
| Enemy belief | The thing the audience currently nods along to | "Using AI more = being ready for AI" |
| The flip | The thesis in three sentences or fewer | Build the human first, then amplify |
| The artefact | ONE of: map, matrix, split, number, teardown, plus what it actually shows | The three-way split |
| Proof | Numbers and claims, EACH flagged verified or unverified | (empty until sourced) |
| Moments | Real named stories | (per story) |
| Hook bank | Your lines verbatim, plus generated candidates | Your three recorded hooks |
| Audience moment | Who feels this and when | The mid-career operator quietly outsourcing their thinking |
| CTA | What the committed do next | Comment / map your bottleneck |

Two rules with teeth:

- **A Story is BUILD-READY only when the sheet is full enough.** The console shows the fill state and will not build a kit from a starved sheet. This is the structural fix for thin concepts producing generic scripts.
- **Every number carries a source flag.** Unverified proof can sit in the sheet, but it cannot enter a script. This is the Sydney lesson made structural: an invented client result nearly went out in public copy this week because nothing in the pipe asked "says who?"

### 3c. The Kit: what one Story produces

Fixed. Not a menu. Designed backwards from the cadence table.

| Asset | Fills | Console does | You do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 long-form script + screen plan | YouTube long, 1 LinkedIn slot | Staged build script, artefact template screens | Record, edit, drop the file back |
| 3 short scripts (3 hooks, one body) | Shorts, IG, TikTok: 9+ slots | Staged build, per-platform captions | Record in the same batch, edit |
| 1 carousel from the artefact | LinkedIn + IG: 2 slots | Fills the artefact template, outputs the PDF | Approve |
| 3 to 4 text posts | LinkedIn: 3 to 4 slots | Drafted from the arc's beats, not summaries | Five minutes of edit each |
| Captions for everything | Every video slot | Automatic, per platform | Glance |
| 1 thumbnail brief | YouTube | First-frame rule until the brand refresh | Pick the frame |
| Polynize page glue | 5 to 7 slots | Repost copy | Approve weekly |

Roughly 12 assets, roughly 25 to 30 scheduled slots, from one Story. The Kit's shape gets reviewed monthly against what earned sends and reshares (the plan already schedules an October "kill the rest" review). It does not get renegotiated per story.

### 3d. The five stations

1. **Catch.** The ideas inbox (already exists). A note typed on your phone in the flow of your week becomes a candidate Story.
2. **Shape.** April interviews you against the Story sheet slots, not freeform. The output is a build-ready sheet, with the fill meter showing what is still missing and the proof flags showing what is still unverified.
3. **Build.** One action: Build the Kit. Scripts through the staged build (your hooks verbatim), carousel and screens from the artefact template, captions, thumbnail brief. Everything lands as a checklist with drafts attached.
4. **Record.** The studio queue (already exists) tells you exactly what to record in one batch. "Waiting on edit" is a real state. Finished edits come back through the media library and attach to their kit items.
5. **Ship.** One screen shows the whole kit spread across the week using your posting-schedule config (already exists). One confirm schedules everything through Metricool. That is your "post it all together at once": one action, one designed wave. Not literally one timestamp.

The dashboard becomes a board: every Story is a card moving Catch → Shape → Build → Record → Ship → **Shipped**. A shipped Story is your archive. This also answers your earlier complete-section-and-archive request, in a better shape than a checkbox.

## 5. Contradictions I have to surface

**C1. You asked for "every possible piece of content automatically", and you also said more-and-more dilutes everything.** Both cannot win. I am siding with your second instinct against your first: the fixed Kit, reviewed monthly. The plan agrees with you, it schedules an October review to kill what did not earn.

**C2. Entertain-the-masses vs the plan's authority goal.** The plan says follower count is not a metric. My recommendation: entertainment lives in the podcast lane, outside the console, for all of Gear 1. If you want that balance changed, it is a leadership-team conversation about the plan, not a build ticket. Your call as founder, but it should be made with Shourov and Kristen, not by the tool.

**C3. The plan holds visual-heavy formats until the brand refresh, and you asked for images now.** Resolution: artefact-led visuals (the map, the split, the number) are substance and ship now with placeholder styling that swaps at the refresh, which matches the standing rebrand note. Glossy image posts wait.

**C4. The plan's cadence depends partly on podcast cutdowns, which you have taken out of the console.** Fine, but then the console owes about 30 of the 50 weekly slots and your podcast lane owes the rest. Stated so the maths stays honest.

**C5. "Works for me first" vs a console already built for five people.** Two streams stay live. Three get parked. Premature multi-tenancy was part of the breadth problem.

**C6. Part of today's verdict was passed on tools that changed yesterday.** The script path you tested is already gone. Fair verdict anyway, because the deeper failure (nothing end to end) stands regardless of any one tool's quality.

## 6. Hard truths

1. **Nothing has ever shipped end to end.** Until one Story goes idea to published, every feature conversation is decoration. This is why the pilot comes before any building.
2. **The tool cannot want it more than the calendar does.** The plan's own top risk: "Marrs gets pulled into delivery and the content stops. Already happened once." No console fixes that. Batching one recording session a week and building four weeks of buffer (the plan's own mitigation) is what fixes that.
3. **The first month will feel slower than the demos did.** Finishing is a toll. The pilot week will be half manual and that is the point: it shows exactly where the tool is weak, with stakes at their lowest.
4. **Killing features you asked for is part of respecting the asks that mattered.** Every parked module was a real request. Parking them is not calling them wrong, it is calling them premature.
5. **The model was never the main problem.** We measured it: your brief was 1% of April's instructions and the concept had no ammunition. Structure fixed that. The model switch exists (SCRIPT_MODEL) and matters at the margin.
6. **Entertainment will not sell mapping sessions in Gear 1.** It might build the audience that buys in Gear 3. Sequence accordingly.
7. **The Sydney number is still unverified and it sits inside your flagship convert story.** Three-day backlog to five-minute loop. If it is not true, it dies today. If it is true, it gets a source and becomes your best proof.
8. **I was the co-author of the sprawl.** The fix for me is a rule, not a vibe: the default answer to new scope is "after four stories", and I will hold it even when it is your idea, which is exactly when holding it matters.
